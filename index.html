About Me

Get to know the person behind the projects.

Hello! I’m Siddheshwari (Siddhi) Bankar

I’m a data scientist, software engineer, and lifelong learner who thrives at the intersection of curiosity and creativity. My journey began in Pune, where I was the kid who always asked “why?”—a habit that’s served me well as I’ve tackled everything from predictive healthcare models to building full-stack web apps.

What sets me apart? I love making sense of messy data and turning it into something that actually matters—whether that’s a dashboard that helps a business make a smarter decision, or a machine learning model that predicts rare events. I’m not just about code; I’m about impact, clarity, and making tech approachable for everyone.

Lately, I’ve been diving deep into the world of AI—exploring everything from generative models to practical applications that make life and work smarter. I’m fascinated by how AI can amplify human creativity and solve real-world problems.

My approach is simple: stay curious, collaborate openly, and never stop learning. I believe the best solutions come from blending technical rigor with a dash of playfulness and a lot of empathy for the end user.
